# Modern FM-1 bank reference: mene311/fm1-banks

- Repository: https://github.com/mene311/fm1-banks
- Audio gallery: https://mene311.github.io/fm1-banks/
- Format: 26 themed banks × 32 six-operator DX7 voices (832 voices)
- Best coverage for our gaps: Synthwave Pad, Lead/Solo, Acid/Techno, Ambient/Texture, Industrial, Sci-Fi/Horror, Misc FX, Electronic Bass, Chiptune.

## Rights

The repository's MIT license explicitly applies to its generator and tooling. Its README says the patch data is curated from public DX7 SysEx collections and remains the work of the original patch authors. Therefore FM1-Lab records this as **reference-only**: audition the gallery and use it to understand modern bank organization, but do not copy or redistribute its bank data unless the relevant patch rights are independently cleared.

## Why it matters

This is the most directly relevant modern FM-1-specific curation project found so far. Its 26 banks are themed for coherent 32-voice collections and include several of the missing musical directions we want. It is a useful benchmark for our own bank balance, naming, and audition workflow.

## Candidate themes to compare against our CC0 archive

- Shimmering / cinematic: Synthwave Pad, Ambient & Texture, Strings & Orchestral
- Modern leads: Lead & Solo, Synthwave Bass
- Electronic / techno: Acid & Techno, Electronic Bass
- Aggressive: Industrial & Metal, Sci-Fi & Horror
- Experimental: Misc FX, Chiptune & Retro Game

The names above are source-bank themes, not evidence that any particular individual patch has been auditioned or approved.

FIRMWARE UNDER TEST

Select from the community firmware catalog or use the emulator's Open control to load an .fwsc package from your computer. M-VAVE stock firmware requires your own downloaded package.

INTERACTION

Use the emulated buttons, encoders and keyboard, or connect a supported MIDI input. The emulator runs the firmware unchanged in WebAssembly and renders its screen and audio.

SAVES & BACKUPS

The emulator saves changed flash locally in the browser. Use its Save & backup controls to export a portable backup. Clearing browser data can remove local saves.

Important boundary: the emulator now runs on the same origin from a pinned upstream MIT-licensed submodule. FM1-Lab can supply documented boot-time input scripts and receive documented try-out lifecycle messages. The Felucca smoke sequence sends MIDI note-on and note-off events; "script sent" confirms delivery to the emulator, not audible output or a semantic firmware pass. Arbitrary internal WASM state and live parameter writes are still not exposed as a stable host API.
